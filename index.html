Questions

Do my photos get uploaded?
No. JuriedIn runs entirely in your browser — your photos never leave your device.
What if I lose my key?
Email Juriedin@proton.me from the address you used and we'll help you recover it.
Which application sites does it cover?
Presets cover ZAPP, CaFÉ, JAS, ShowSubmit and EntryThingy, and you can save your own settings for anything else.
What formats can I export?
JPEG, PNG and WebP, at the size and quality each show asks for.
